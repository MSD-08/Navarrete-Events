The studio

A design studio for celebration

Navarrete Events is an event planning and design studio based in Tijuana, Baja California. We create weddings, quinceañeras, graduations and private celebrations in which every decision — from the light to the last piece of cutlery — answers to an idea.

Philosophy

We don’t set out to make a celebration look luxurious. We want it to feel inevitable — as if it could not have been any other way.

Every project begins with a conversation and a great many questions. We want to understand the people celebrating: their story, their taste, the way they wish to welcome their guests. From there we build a concept of their own — never a template.

Then comes the least visible and most important work: floor plans, budgets, suppliers, timings and the build. That is where a beautiful idea becomes an evening that works.

Principles

  1. I

    Concept before decoration

    Every element answers to a central idea. If something doesn’t add to the story, it doesn’t come in.

  2. II

    Light as a material

    We design lighting from the outset: it decides how materials, colours and people look.

  3. III

    Scale and detail

    Installations that transform a space and, at the same time, a table that rewards a closer look.

  4. IV

    Personal, not literal

    We interpret tastes and stories with elegance, never with the obvious.

  5. V

    Flawless execution

    A clear plan, coordinated suppliers and a team present from beginning to end.

The team

A team present in every moment

On the day, our team coordinates suppliers, timings and the build so that everything happens as planned, with discretion and calm.
