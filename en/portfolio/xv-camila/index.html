Quinceañera Camila

Tunnels of flowers

Floral tunnels and arches, glass tables and a palette of rose, gold and violet.

Celebration
Quinceañera
Photographs
36

Concept

An immersive celebration in which flowers become architecture. Tunnels covered entirely in roses lead to the dance floor; concentric arches frame the stage and a suspended wave of blossoms crosses the room. On the tables, crystal, gold, candelabra and pale-pink arrangements temper the intensity of the colour.
