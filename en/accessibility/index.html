Accessibility

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to discover our work and contact us with ease. This website was designed with reference to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

What we have considered

Semantic structure and ordered headings; full keyboard navigation with visible focus; alternative text for photographs; sufficient colour contrast; forms with clear labels, instructions and error messages; controls to pause the home page image sequence; respect for the system preference to reduce motion; and a layout that works on phones, tablets and computers.

The videos on this site have no audio and are visual fragments of events; they only play when you choose.

Found a barrier?

If any part of the site is difficult to use, please message us on Instagram at @navarrete.events or through the contact form. We will help you through whichever channel you prefer and fix the problem.
