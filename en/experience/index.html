The experience

From the first meeting to the last light of the night

Planning an important celebration shouldn’t feel like a to-do list. Our process is designed so every decision makes sense, every supplier has a clear direction and you can enjoy the journey.

The process

  1. 01

    1. The first conversation

    We listen before we propose. We talk about the date, the venue, the guests and the investment — but above all about you: what moves you, what you want guests to feel, and what you’d rather not repeat.

  2. 02

    2. Vision & creative direction

    We translate that conversation into a concept: palette, materials, light, flowers and atmosphere. A complete visual proposal that becomes the compass for everything that follows.

  3. 03

    3. Planning & curation

    Budget, timeline and suppliers. We select and coordinate every partner so that all of them work toward the same idea, with clear schedules and responsibilities.

  4. 04

    4. Design development & coordination

    Floor plans, mock-ups, table samples and stationery details. What was an intention in the concept becomes measurement, material and logistics.

  5. 05

    5. Production & build

    On the day, our team directs the complete build: structure, lighting, flowers and tables. Every element finds its place, in order and without surprises.

  6. 06

    6. The celebration

    We quietly coordinate the timings, the suppliers and every unexpected moment. All you have to do is be present and live what you imagined.
